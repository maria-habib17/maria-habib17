## 🔬 Featured Research — SimProbe

> **A controlled experimental framework for studying robustness and discrimination in source-code similarity.**

**SimProbe** investigates what happens to similarity measurements when programs undergo controlled transformations.

```text
Source Program
      │
      ▼
Controlled Transformation
      │
      ├── Identifier Rename
      ├── Method Reorder
      └── Class Split
      │
      ▼
Program Representation
      │
      ▼
Similarity Comparison
      │
      ▼
Robustness × Discrimination
```

### Research focus

`Program Representation` • `Structural Similarity` • `Controlled Experiments` • `Reproducibility`

The central question:

> **Can a representation become more robust to source-code transformations without simply making unrelated programs look more similar?**
